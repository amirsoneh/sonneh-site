About SONNEH.

SONNEH is an independent design studio based in Perth, Western Australia, specialising in interior design, concept development and design strategy.

Led by Amir Sonneh, the studio draws on international experience across Australia, Iran and Italy, spanning more than 40 residential, commercial and heritage projects.

Our approach begins with understanding people: how they live, work and interact with the spaces around them. We explore the relationship between human behaviour, spatial identity, materials and function.

From early concepts to considered design solutions, SONNEH brings together creative thinking and strategic insight to transform complexity into clarity.

AMIR SONNEH Concept Designer & Design Strategist Perth, Western Australia
