About

SONNEH is an independent design studio based in Perth, Western Australia, focused on concept design, interior design and design strategy.

Led by Amir Sonneh, the studio draws on international experience across three continents, spanning more than 40 residential, commercial and heritage projects.

Our approach explores the relationship between people, space, materials and identity.
